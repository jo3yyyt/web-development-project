3. What did you find challenging: tell us what was hard about completing the project. This might be technical (understanding the API) or organizational (communication within the group).

Among the challenges we faced while working on the project, Joey faced difficulty on getting the mood widget integrated into the whole website. It was easy to create the mood widget itself, thanks to readily available AI technologies and online resources. However, the integration process was still challenging.  At first, Joey assumed that the `index.html` file and `mood-widget.js` were the only files that needed to be updated, so she concentrated entirely on those tasks. She didn't think that in order to properly integrate the widget into the website, other files and parameters required to be altered. The widget did not show up correctly within the overall website structure as a result of this mistake. To integrate the widget, a deeper understanding of the relationships between different codebase components was required, how the mood widget interacted with other components. It's also a bit difficult to integrate the whole project together as in the beginning some of us accidentally change some of part of the file which caused unwanted bug. We had to do a lot of testing to identify and fix bugs, which required a firm understanding of responsive design principles and cross-browser compatibility issues.  Troubleshooting difficulties that emerged throughout the integration process was laborious and frequently necessitated navigating numerous files and coding layers, making the task much more challenging.

Effective communication within the group was crucial, and fortunately, all team members were very supportive. We tried to promptly answered the questions and help to troubleshoot some issues. In order to make sure that every component of the project worked together seamlessly, team coordination was crucial. 

Overall, while the actual development of the widget and taskcard was manageable due to available resources, integrating it smoothly into the existing website framework posed an early challenge when we starting. It highlighted the importance of understanding the entire project’s architecture and maintaining clear and detailed communication within the team to ensure all parts work seamlessly together. This experience underscored the need for thorough planning, effective communication, and a collaborative approach to completing a team project.
